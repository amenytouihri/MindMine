# MindMine | Research and Data Analysis

This directory contains analytical work supporting **MindMine**, a research-led exploration of digital support for paediatric speech and language therapy.

## Research questions

The stakeholder research explored:

- How SLTs allocate time across clinical and administrative activities.
- How they would prefer to allocate that time.
- Barriers to engaging families in home-based therapy activities.
- Opportunities and constraints for a digital therapist–family platform.

## Stakeholders

The wider discovery work involved SLTs, parents and schools. The SLT survey included 25 completed responses out of 42 recorded responses; item-level sample sizes vary because not every respondent answered every question. A school stakeholder sample of nine was also recorded in the project research.

> Before publication, verify the survey denominator, recruitment methods, eligibility criteria and whether all recorded responses were eligible for analysis.

## Selected SLT findings

| Measure | Current median | Ideal median | Paired n | Reported test result |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Family engagement time | 13% | 38% | 19 | z = 2.066, p = .039, r = .47 |
| Report-writing time | 38% | 13% | 20 | z = -2.324, p = .020, r = .52 |

These figures reflect reported results from the project's analysis. Confirm test choice, calculation method, missing-data handling and direction of differences against the underlying notebooks or scripts before citing them in academic work.

## Interpretation

The responses suggest a difference between the current and preferred distribution of SLT time, particularly for family engagement and report writing. They motivate further investigation of digital tools that support families and simplify workflows.

These findings do **not** establish that the proposed application improves clinical outcomes, reduces documentation time or is effective in practice.

6. Statistical tests, assumptions and effect-size calculations.
